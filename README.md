# The World in One Picture — Wednesday 18 November 2026

A broadsheet front page that sums up the whole of Wednesday 18 November 2026 in a single 1024×1024 image: six lead stories drawn as bold flat-vector scenes, plus an "Also Today" strip with three more.

The leads: the Trump–Xi–Putin summit in Shenzhen; Iran and the US trading blows across the Gulf; Russian strikes on Odesa's historic centre; Hakeem Jeffries nominated for Speaker in Washington; Nvidia's soaring earnings and its board shutting the door on Jensen Huang; and two armies massing on the Ethiopia–Eritrea border. Every story was cross-checked in at least two reputable outlets reporting that day, and the page names the day in its masthead.

`main.ts` copies the shipped `entry.png` to the output directory, byte for byte.
